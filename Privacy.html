Privacy Policy

PrintersClan, accessible at www.printersclan.com, is committed to safeguarding the privacy of its visitors and users. This summary outlines the key provisions of the Privacy Policy governing data collection, use, and protection on the website.

Scope and Consent

This policy applies exclusively to information collected through online activities on the PrintersClan website. By accessing or continuing to use the site, users provide their consent to the terms herein.

Categories of Information Collected

Voluntarily provided information:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Telephone Number
  • Company Name
  • Postal Address
  • Message Content
  • Attachments
  • Information submitted through contact forms, account registration, or service inquiries

Automatically collected information:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type and Version
  • Internet Service Provider
  • Date and Time of Access
  • Referring and Exit Pages
  • Pages Visited
  • Approximate Click Counts

Such data are collected through standard server logs and analytics tools, are not linked to personally identifiable information, and are used for site administration, trend analysis, and aggregate demographic insights.

Purpose of Data Use

Collected information is utilized solely to:

  • Operate, maintain, and enhance the website and help services
  • Personalize user experience and provide relevant technical guidance
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve functionality and develop new features
  • Respond to inquiries, deliver service notifications, and send account-related communications
  • Identify, prevent, and mitigate fraud, security incidents, or technical issues
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

PrintersClan does not sell personal information to third parties.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The website and authorized third-party partners (including advertising networks) may employ cookies, web beacons, JavaScript, and similar technologies to enhance user experience, personalize content, and measure advertising effectiveness.

These technologies may collect IP addresses automatically. Users retain control over cookies through their browser settings; however, disabling cookies may restrict certain site functionalities.

Third-party practices are governed by their respective privacy policies, which users are advised to review.

Third-Party Advertising

PrintersClan collaborates with external ad servers and networks whose privacy policies independently regulate their use of cookies and data for ad targeting and performance measurement.

PrintersClan neither controls nor has access to such third-party cookies.

Privacy and Data Subject Rights

CCPA / CPRA

California residents have rights to request disclosure of categories and specific personal information collected, deletion of personal data (subject to statutory exceptions), and opt-out of any sale of personal information.

Requests are processed within statutory timeframes without discrimination.

GDPR

Users may exercise rights including:

  • Access
  • Rectification
  • Erasure
  • Restriction of Processing
  • Objection to Processing
  • Data Portability

Access to copies may involve a reasonable administrative fee. All requests receive a response within one month.

To exercise these rights, users should contact PrintersClan using the details provided below.

Protection of Children's Privacy

The website does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13.

Should such information be identified, prompt efforts will be made to delete it. Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor children's online activities.

Security

Appropriate technical and organizational measures are implemented to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Nonetheless, no method of electronic transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed entirely secure.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be amended periodically. Revised versions will be posted on the website with an updated effective date.

Continued use of the site following such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
